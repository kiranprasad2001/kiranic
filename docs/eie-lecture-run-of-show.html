Q&A — 8 answers already prepped in the script

Will AI replace instrumentation engineers?No. Someone has to decide where the sensor goes and what happens when it's wrong.

Why not deep learning on raw signal?You'd need failure data nobody has, and you can't explain it to a maintenance manager.

Is my curriculum outdated?Signal processing and control aren't. The software/data layer is the gap — 40 hrs of work.

How much math?Linear algebra + probability, solidly. Enough calculus for optimization. No measure theory.

Twin for a small plant?Start with the cheapest thing that changes a decision. Usually 3 well-placed sensors, not a twin.

You were negative about twins. Expect this. Negative about surrogates trained on healthy data. Not about physics-model twins — demo two is one.

LLMs in industry?Useful for unstructured text (maintenance logs, procedures). Not for vibration signals.

You haven't worked in a plant. Expect this — take head-on. Check my arithmetic. Organizational patterns transfer. Anything specific to commissioning, ask someone else.

If stumped: “I don't know. That's outside what I've actually done.” Then move on.
